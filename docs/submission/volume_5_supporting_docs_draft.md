## 8. Disclosure of Information (DFARS 252.204-7000)

### Fundamental Research Disclosure

Techman Studios requests to identify the proposed Phase I research as **fundamental research** to eliminate the requirements for prior approval of public disclosure of information (DFARS 252.204-7000).

*   **Objective**: Demonstrate the mathematical and software feasibility of using a shared contextual state-space for multi-agent event fusion and explanation.
*   **Scope of Work**: The Phase I scope consists entirely of unclassified software simulations, synthetic scenario generation, and baseline comparisons. It does not involve real-world mission data, classified systems, or operational targeting.
*   **Justification**: The research is basic and applied scientific inquiry conducted at private facilities, intended for eventual dissemination in scientific publications and academic reviews. It does not contain proprietary military secrets or ITAR-controlled operational parameters.
