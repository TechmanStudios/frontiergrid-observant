# Anticipated Benefits

## Naval Benefits

- Earlier detection of anomalous cross-domain event clusters.
- Better separation of event risk from data-quality risk.
- Replayable explanations that support analyst review and chain-of-command trust.
- Reduced overconfidence through skeptic-agent review and uncertainty scoring.
- A repeatable T&E harness for evaluating human-AI teaming and risk escalation controls.

## Technical Benefits

- Adapter-first design that allows deterministic fallback behavior while preserving a path to Frontier_OS / Exciton-MoA experiments.
- Common event and alert schemas suitable for synthetic scenarios and later approved datasets.
- Baseline comparison against rule-only and centralized orchestration approaches.

## Dual-Use Benefits

- Maritime and logistics anomaly detection.
- Critical infrastructure monitoring.
- Emergency response situational awareness.
- Enterprise risk intelligence and source-grounded operations dashboards.
