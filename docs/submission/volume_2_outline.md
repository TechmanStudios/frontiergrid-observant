# Volume 2 Technical Volume Outline

The DON Phase I Technical Volume limit is 10 pages. Keep this outline tight and evidence-driven.

1. Identification and Significance of the Problem or Opportunity
2. Phase I Technical Objectives
3. Phase I Statement of Work
4. Related Work
5. Relationship with Future Research or Research and Development
6. Commercialization Strategy
7. Key Personnel
8. Foreign Citizens
9. Facilities / Equipment
10. Subcontractors / Consultants
11. Prior, Current, or Pending Support of Similar Proposals or Awards
12. Identification and Assertion of Restrictions on Government Use, Release, or Disclosure of Technical Data or Computer Software

## Reviewer-Facing Emphasis

- Technical merit is the primary evaluation factor.
- Explain key distinctions from current centralized ISRT exploitation systems.
- Show a clear six-month Base and six-month Option.
- Map every prototype feature to a topic requirement, metric, or deliverable.
- Use measured feasibility language, not operational-readiness claims.
