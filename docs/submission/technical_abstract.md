# Technical Abstract Draft

FrontierGrid-Observant is an unclassified Phase I feasibility testbed for risk-aware regenerative AI-based multimodal visual-tactical monitoring, understanding, alerting, and assistance. Naval mission environments produce fragmented, time-sensitive data across sensors, operators, networks, documents, and autonomous systems. Existing centralized AI orchestration can become brittle when events are incomplete, contradictory, degraded, delayed, or distributed across domains.

Techman Studios proposes to investigate a federated contextual-state approach that adapts prior SOL, Frontier_OS, and Exciton-MoA research into an Observant-AI-style prototype. The Phase I effort will build at least three synthetic cross-domain challenge problems, a common event schema, lightweight mission-focused agent roles, a deterministic fallback reasoning substrate, risk scoring and escalation controls, replayable explanations, and a test-and-evaluation harness. The current testbed includes five synthetic scenarios whose anomaly evidence rotates across Sea, Space, Air, Land, and Cyber style domains, including an unknown-unknown injection case that introduces a novel weak-signal disturbance into the continuous state-space stream. The prototype will compare shared-context behavior against rule-based and centralized baselines using sensitivity, specificity, precision, miss rate, false discovery rate, false omission rate, analytic completeness, uniqueness, validity, consistency, accuracy under noisy data, context-geodesic alignment, active-domain coverage, contribution balance, and candidate-team completeness.

The expected result is a controlled feasibility demonstration showing whether multi-agent contextual fusion can improve risk-aware alerting and source-grounded explanation without requiring classified data in Phase I. Dual-use applications include maritime logistics anomaly detection, critical infrastructure monitoring, emergency response situational awareness, and enterprise risk intelligence.
