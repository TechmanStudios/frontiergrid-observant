# Phase I Submission Checklist

Working checklist for DON26BZ01-NV023, based on the local topic PDF and DON Phase I submission instructions. Verify each item against the active DSIP posting before submission.

## Core Submission Answer

No deployed app is required for the Phase I proposal submission unless DSIP or the topic posting adds a specific attachment requirement. The submission package is the proposal volume set in DSIP. The demo app and generated artifacts are useful for creating screenshots, tables, and confidence in the technical narrative, but the proposal should stand on its own inside the required volumes.

## Official Submission Path

- Register in DSIP early and confirm the Corporate Official can certify the proposal before close.
- Submit through DSIP only; proposals sent by other means are disregarded.
- Confirm all PDFs/uploads are unlocked, unencrypted, and not password protected.
- Confirm the email address on the Proposal Cover Sheet is correct.
- Register for the DSIP listserv and monitor the topic page for amendments, Q&A updates, and deadline changes.

## Volume 2 Compliance Checks

- Maximum 10 pages, regardless of content.
- Single column.
- Single spaced.
- Standard 8.5 x 11 pages.
- One-inch margins on all sides.
- No body font smaller than 10 point.
- Include both a six-month Phase I Base and a six-month Phase I Option.
- Clearly identify Base tasks and Option tasks.
- Keep the narrative self-contained; reviewers may not consider extra technical reports in Volume 5.
- Avoid operational-readiness claims. Frame the work as an unclassified Phase I feasibility effort.

## Cost Volume Checks

- Phase I Base <= $140,000.
- Phase I Option <= $100,000.
- Base and Option costs separated and clearly identified.
- No cost sharing for DON Phase I.
- At least two-thirds of the work must be performed by the proposing small business in both Base and Option.
- Include enough detail for labor, subcontractor, material, travel, and other direct costs.
- Consider whether to request Phase I TABA, up to $6,500, only if there is a clear commercialization/IP/vendor-support need.

## Topic-Specific Technical Checks

- Address CMMC Level 2 (Self) planning without overpromising final certification status.
- Treat the topic as ITAR/EAR-sensitive and keep the proposal unclassified.
- Disclose any foreign nationals if applicable; if none, say none consistently.
- Motivate the design with three challenge problems supported by relevant synthetic or approved datasets.
- Explain clear distinctions from current state-of-the-art naval ISRT information exploitation systems.
- Cover cross-domain contextual collaborative learning, inference, recognition, teaming, adaptation, cause/effect sensitivity, confidence in TTP enhancement/risk reduction, AI-risk escalation control, and human responsiveness.
- Include the Phase I performance criteria: sensitivity, specificity, precision, miss rate, false discovery rate, and false omission rate.
- Include explanation-quality metrics: analytic completeness, uniqueness, validity, consistency, and accuracy under noisy data.
- Map Phase I outputs to the topic deliverables: initial prototype technology, T&E, demonstration, Phase II plan, and final report.

## Use Of The Demo App

- Keep the static dashboard as internal supporting evidence and a possible private demo surface.
- Do not make the deployed app the core submission artifact.
- Use generated artifacts to produce concise Volume 2 evidence: scenario table, metric table, and role-evidence summary.
- Do not place extensive test data, screenshots, or technical reports in Volume 5 to extend the 10-page limit.
- Revisit data-rights, export-control, and runtime-dependency decisions before sharing a public or customer-accessible demo.
