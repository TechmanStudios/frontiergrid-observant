# Compliance Notes

These notes summarize repo-impacting constraints from the local DON26BZ01-NV023 topic PDF, Navy Phase I instructions, and DoW SBIR BAA preface. Verify against the official solicitation before submission.

## Data Classification

- Phase I repository work should use unclassified synthetic, public mock, or sanitized OSINT-style data.
- Do not store classified material, CUI, sensitive operational data, real mission data, or credentials in this repo.
- Classified proposals are not accepted under the DoW SBIR Program. The topic notes that Phase II work may become classified.

## CMMC and NIST

- The topic projects CMMC Level 2 (Self).
- Level 2 readiness involves NIST SP 800-171 controls, applicable self-assessment or assessment requirements, and annual affirmation according to the solicitation.
- The Navy instructions also reference current NIST SP 800-171 assessment upload to SPRS for award consideration.

## Export Controls

- The topic is marked ITAR/EAR restricted.
- Treat technical content as export-control-sensitive until reviewed.
- If any proposed effort is subject to ITAR, the solicitation references DD Form 2345 / JCP evidence in Volume 5.
- If any proposed effort is subject to EAR, export licensing through BIS SNAP-R may be required.

## Proposal and Deliverables

- DON Phase I Technical Volume is limited to 10 pages and must include a six-month Base and six-month Option.
- Technical merit is most important, followed by key personnel and commercialization potential.
- Phase I contract deliverables are typically kick-off brief, progress reports, and final report.
- Final reports require SF 298, topic-identifying abstract language, and distribution markings assigned by the sponsoring activity.

## Data Rights and Proprietary Markings

- Volume 2 should include identification and assertion of restrictions on government use, release, or disclosure of technical data or computer software.
- Volume 5 may include technical data-rights assertions and related administrative documents.
- Unmarked proprietary proposal information may not be protected. Mark restricted proposal pages according to the BAA instructions.
- SBIR technical data/software developed under award generally remains with the contractor while the U.S. Government receives a royalty-free government-purpose license during the protected period described by the applicable DFARS clause.

## Phase I Risk Avoidance

- Avoid human subjects, animal testing, recombinant DNA, and GFE dependencies unless absolutely necessary.
- Avoid unsupported operational claims.
- Keep hardware asks out of Phase I unless directly required by the work plan.
